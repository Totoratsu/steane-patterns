Steane Patterns

Two logical qubits, each a Steane [[7,1,3]] code, under repeated error correction. Each row is one round of the 12 parity checks; the last row is the 14 data qubits read out at the end. Noise lights the checks up, and each lit check plays a note. Two decoders read the pattern: plain BP and Relay-BP.

Ground truth comes from tracking the Pauli frame of the injected noise, so every shot is judged exactly. The decoders get a phenomenological model; for SI1000 that model is approximate (hook errors are left out), just like a real decoder with an imperfect noise model.
